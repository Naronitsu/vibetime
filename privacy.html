Privacy

VibeTime is a free time tracker. This page explains what happens to your data. Back to VibeTime

Your data stays with you

The hours you log, your settings and your allowances are stored in your own browser (local storage). VibeTime has no server and no accounts, and it does not collect, sell or share your data. There are no analytics or advertising trackers.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, VibeTime asks for one permission: access to its own hidden app-data folder in your Drive (drive.appdata). It saves one file there, vibetime.json, containing the same data that is in your browser, so your devices can stay in step.

VibeTime cannot see or change any of your other files in Google Drive, and the file is not visible in your normal Drive. Your data goes from your browser straight to your own Google Drive, and nobody else, including the author of VibeTime, receives it. You can stop at any time with Disconnect in Settings, and remove VibeTime's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's hidden data is also possible from there.

BambooHR browser extension (optional)

If you install the VibeTime for BambooHR extension, it reads the timesheet you are looking at when you click its button and passes the days to a VibeTime tab in your own browser. It does not use your BambooHR password or API key, does not contact any server, and the days are discarded after a few minutes if not collected. VibeTime shows a preview and saves nothing until you confirm. If you leave “Follow my BambooHR clock” on, the extension also tells VibeTime when you punch in or out (the times and the day’s total only); this is switched off in Settings, and every change can be undone.

Other services the site talks to

  • Public holidays: if you pick a country other than Malta, VibeTime asks date.nager.at for that country's holidays. Only the country code and the year are sent.
  • Fonts: the page loads its fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs of page visits.
